# English writing guide

Short commerce English. Not SaaS, not crypto ops.

## Preferred

New payment · Paid ✓ · Waiting for payment · Needs attention · Customers · Where should payments arrive? · Payment link

## Avoid in primary UI

Create payment intent · Transaction finalized · GMV · AOV · Checkout defaults · Physical fulfillment · TRC-20 / BEP-20 (those belong in Details)

## Mechanics

- Labels: 1–3 words
- Errors: what happened, money safety, next step
- Don’t title-case every phrase (“Needs Attention” → “Needs attention”)
