# Pooli brand book

## Brand idea

Pooli makes getting paid from a DM feel as simple as sending a message.

## Product promise

Crypto complexity disappears. The seller sees **Paid ✓**.

## Personality

Simple · Warm · Confident · Trustworthy · Modern

Not: crypto-neon, SaaS-dashboard, bureaucratic, trendy-for-a-season.

## Logo

Runtime marks live in `apps/web/public/brand/` and `apps/web/src/components/BrandMark.tsx`.

- Primary wordmark: Pooli (EN, DM Sans) / پولی (FA, Peyda Black)
- Icon/mark: mint leaf-like P mark on light or brand field
- Clear space: at least the cap-height of the wordmark on all sides
- Minimum size: 24px icon; 80px wide wordmark on screen
- Don’t: recolor to Apple blue, add coin/Bitcoin motifs, stretch, add drop shadows, write پولیی
