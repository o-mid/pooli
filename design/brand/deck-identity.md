# Deck identity

Two decks, two jobs. They are not clones with a title swap.

| | Investor | Partner |
|--|----------|---------|
| Job | Explain the product and the stage honestly | Show where Pooli plugs into their world |
| Ask | Conversation — not a fake raise number | Integration / intro / operational yes |
| Proof | Live product + labeled demo | What’s ENABLED vs PLANNED |
| Forbidden | Fake TAM, GMV, logos, “the team of 40” | Promising Instagram-on or SMS-on |

## Look

- Ground `#F2F5F3` or white. Ink `#0B1F1A`. Mint only for Paid ✓ and section ticks
- Display: DM Sans (EN) / Peyda (FA titles if a FA deck is cut)
- One sentence per slide if possible. 16:9
- Real product screenshots, demo data only
- Slide numbers small, secondary ink
- No stock “global network” maps, no coin heroes

## Type scale on a 1920 × 1080 frame

- Title 56–72px
- Body 22–28px
- Caption 16–18px
- Margins ≥ 72px

## Required honesty slides

1. What we will not claim (traction empty on purpose)
2. What’s on vs off (TRON + BSC + Telegram + email on; Instagram + phone OTP off)
