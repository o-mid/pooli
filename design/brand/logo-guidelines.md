# Logo guidelines

Source of truth for in-app rendering: [`BrandMark`](../../apps/web/src/components/BrandMark.tsx).

## Variants

- Color on light (`--bg` / white)
- On-dark / on-brand (white mark)
- Monochrome ink
- FA wordmark (Peyda) vs EN wordmark (DM Sans)

## Clear space & size

Keep a quiet margin around the mark. Don’t lock it in a heavy card or gradient.

App icon uses the mark on brand mint `#0F8F6B`.

## Incorrect usage

- Imitating Apple, Stripe, or wallet-app chrome
- Putting TRON/Bitcoin logos next to the wordmark
- Outlining, rotating, or dual-tone neon
- Translating the wordmark letter-by-letter into a new Latin font for Persian
