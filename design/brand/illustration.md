# Illustration

Small, warm, recognizable. Support the idea: **money arriving simply**.

## Do

- Quiet scenes: a link, a check, a phone, a shop counter
- Flat or lightly dimensional, Pooli mint + ink
- Persian-first compositions when the audience is FA

## Don’t

- Floating coins, Bitcoin logos, astronauts
- Generic 3D fintech blobs
- Neon, glass stacks, dashboard mock clutter

Production files: `.local-brand-studio/illustrations/` (gitignored).
