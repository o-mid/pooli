## Sizes (verified 15 Aug 2026)

Sources: Buffer “Social Media Image Sizes in 2026”; Instagram 4:5 still the scheduler-safe feed; LinkedIn company vs personal banners differ.

| Surface | Size | Notes |
|---------|------|--------|
| IG feed / carousel | **1080 × 1350** (4:5) | Default. Grid now previews closer to 3:4; 4:5 still uploads everywhere |
| IG grid-only puzzle | 1080 × 1440 (3:4) | Manual upload; some schedulers reject |
| IG / Telegram story + Reel | **1080 × 1920** | Keep type out of ~220 top / 280 bottom |
| IG profile photo | 320 × 320 min | Circle crop — center the mark |
| LinkedIn personal banner | **1584 × 396** | Photo overlaps lower-left on desktop |
| LinkedIn company banner | **1128 × 191** | Very shallow; wordmark + one short line |
| LinkedIn profile / company photo | 400 × 400 | Circle / rounded |
| LinkedIn feed landscape | 1200 × 627 | Link-style |
| LinkedIn feed portrait | 1080 × 1350 | More height in feed |
| X avatar | 400 × 400 | Circle |

- Mark 48–64px, radius 16
- One idea per frame
- Product shots labeled demo
