# Photography

Prefer real-feeling social-commerce moments: a seller’s phone, a product on a table, a quiet shop — not stock “crypto trader” imagery.

- Light, natural, slightly cool-green to sit on `--bg`
- Faces optional; if used, Iranian everyday context, not luxury-fintech posing
- Never overlay real customer PII
- Product screenshots in marketing must use the demo merchant (تهران اسنیکرز), never production data
