# Product principles

**Complexity belongs inside Pooli, not in front of the user.**

Pooli is for Instagram and Telegram sellers. They should not need fintech, crypto, SaaS, or blockchain knowledge.

## Promise

Create a payment → send a link → customer pays → seller sees **Paid ✓**.

## Rules

1. Having a feature does not mean it deserves a navigation item.
2. Default UI is the minimum needed for the next decision.
3. When uncertain between showing and hiding, hide behind progressive disclosure.
4. Commerce language, not infrastructure language.
5. A returning merchant can create and share a payment in three actions: **New payment → amount → Share**.
6. Payment correctness is frozen. Visual work must not rewrite matching, reservation, confirmation, or settlement.

## Personality

Simple, warm, confident, trustworthy, modern — closer to Apple Wallet than a crypto dashboard.
