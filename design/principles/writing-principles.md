# Writing principles

Write like an Iranian product designer (FA) or a calm commerce product (EN). Not like translated software.

## Do

- Short verbs: پرداخت جدید, Share, Save
- Status people say out loud: پرداخت شد ✓, منتظر پرداخت, نیاز به بررسی
- One idea per sentence
- Tell people if their money is safe when something goes wrong

## Don’t

- Software calques: مشاهده موارد نیازمند بررسی, تنظیمات چک‌اوت
- Crypto in primary UI: TRC-20, transaction hash, payment intent, RPC
- Brand misspelling: پولیی (always **پولی**)
- Fake analytics copy

## Brand name

- FA: پولی
- EN: Pooli
