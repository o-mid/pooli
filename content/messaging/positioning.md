## 30 seconds

### Founder (EN) — winner

I built Pooli for people who sell in DMs. You agree a price, you send a link, they pay, you see Paid ✓. The money goes to your wallet — we never hold it. That’s the whole product.

### Founder (FA) — winner

پولی برای کسیه که تو دایرکت می‌فروشه. روی مبلغ توافق می‌کنی، یه لینک می‌فرستی، طرف پرداخت می‌کنه، تو پرداخت شد ✓ رو می‌بینی. پول می‌ره کیف خودت. ما وسط نیستیم.

### Rejected 30s

- “We’re building the payment rail for Iranian social commerce.” — category fog.
- “Non-custodial USDT checkout on TRON and BNB Smart Chain.” — true, wrong room.

## 60 seconds

### Founder (EN) — winner

If you sell on Instagram or Telegram, the unpaid part of the job is usually the receipt. Someone says they paid. You hunt the chat. You zoom a screenshot. You still aren’t sure.

Pooli is a page you send instead. You type the amount in toman. They open the link and pay USDT to the address you set. We watch the payment and mark Paid ✓ only when it’s actually there.

I’m not asking you to become a crypto person. I’m asking you to send a link.

### Founder (FA) — winner

اگه اینستاگرام یا تلگرام می‌فروشی، سخت‌ترین قسمت کار معمولاً خودِ جنسه نیست — رسیدشه. طرف می‌گه واریز کردم. تو چت را می‌گردی. اسکرین‌شات را بزرگ می‌کنی. هنوز مطمئن نیستی.

پولی همون صفحه است که به‌جاش می‌فرستی. مبلغ را به تومان می‌زنی. مشتری لینک را باز می‌کند و به آدرسی که خودت گذاشتی واریز می‌کند. ما صبر می‌کنیم تا پرداخت واقعاً برسد؛ بعد پرداخت شد ✓.

نمی‌خوام کریپتوکار بشوی. می‌خوام لینک بفرستی.

### Notes

No origin myth. No “I used to run a shop.” If a real story is added later, it goes here only after it’s true.

## By audience

### Investor

**1-line:** Checkout for Iranian social-commerce — DM order in, Paid ✓ out.

**30s:** Sellers already close orders in Instagram and Telegram. They do not have a till. Pooli is a non-custodial checkout page: toman in, exact USDT to the merchant wallet, server-side Paid. Live on pooli.shop, TRON and BNB Chain enabled. We will not show you a fake TAM or a merchant count we haven’t published.

**Do not:** slides titled “$4.2B opportunity” without a source. Do not put “traction” as a vanity number.

### Partner (Telegram, Meta, wallet, agency)

**1-line:** We sit in the gap between “I’ll take it” and Paid ✓.

**30s:** Your users already sell in chat. We give them a link to send and a status they can trust. Telegram bot is on. Instagram composer is built and not on for customers yet. We will not pretend otherwise.

This deck is operational, not a fundraise.

### Merchant

**1-line:** Send the link. That’s it.

**30s:** مبلغ را بزن. لینک را بفرست. وقتی پرداخت شد، خودت می‌فهمی. پول می‌رود کیف خودت.

No chain names unless they ask “where does the money go?”

### Buyer

**1-line:** Open the link. Pay. Done.

**30s:** This page is from the shop you already talked to. Pay the exact amount. You’ll see the status update. You don’t need a Pooli account.

Never pitch Pooli-the-startup to a buyer.

### Technical

**1-line:** Unique-amount USDT matching. Server-only PAID. TRON + BSC.

**30s:** Merchant amount is toman. Payable is exact USDT per destination + network + token while the reservation is live. Adapters normalize transfers; the worker confirms; the browser cannot mark paid. SSE is process-local; Postgres is the source of truth. Instagram flag is off. Phone OTP is mock-only.

## Competitive frame (honest, no theater)

We have not published a competitor matrix. Names that appear in the old spec (Payli, tringPay, BTCPay, Paycilo) are **research notes**, not a claim that we beat them.

If asked “why you?”:

1. The job is the DM, not a dashboard.
2. Money never sits with us.
3. Paid ✓ is a server fact, not a screenshot.

If we cannot say those three without stretching, we say less.
