# Banned phrases

If a draft needs one of these to land, rewrite the draft.

## Invented proof

- trusted by thousands / hundreds of sellers
- used by leading brands
- $X GMV / AOV / processed
- named merchants who did not ask to be named
- fake quotes, fake DMs presented as real
- “our investors” / “backed by”
- a TAM / SAM / SOM number we did not source
- a legal entity we have not filled in

## Web3 / SaaS fog

- revolutionary infrastructure
- the future of payments
- next-gen / cutting-edge / seamless / frictionless
- unlock / supercharge / empower
- web3-native / on-chain experience (in merchant or buyer copy)
- permissionless commerce
- “just works” as a substitute for saying what happens
- game-changer / disrupt / category-defining
- AI-powered (we are not)

## Crypto in the first sentence

Do not lead merchant or social copy with:

- TRC-20 / BEP-20 / RPC / confirmation depth
- payment intent / reservation / matcher
- non-custodial (say “money goes to your wallet”)
- hash / txid (belongs under Payment details)
- gas / gwei
- “USDT on TRON and BNB Smart Chain” as the hero — that is a footnote, not the product

Technical audiences may hear the precise words. Posters and bios may not.

## Tone we are not

- پولیی (always **پولی**)
- مشاهده موارد نیازمند بررسی
- تنظیمات چک‌اوت
- “Create Payment Intent”
- Needs Attention (title case)
- 🚀🔥💯 emoji walls
- “Link in bio ✨ let’s gooo”
- “Excited to announce” / “Thrilled to share” / “We’re live!” with no payload
- “DM me if you want access” as a fake scarcity play
- Full-mint posters except Paid ✓ / a single CTA story
- Coin illustrations, Bitcoin marks, astronauts, neon grids

## Over-claiming the stack

- “realtime infrastructure” (SSE is process-local)
- “bank-grade” / “military-grade”
- “instant settlement” (confirmations take time; we show Detected → Confirming → Paid)
- “works with every wallet”
- “Instagram payments” while `INSTAGRAM_ENABLED=false`
- “phone login” in production

## Soft replacements

|-------|----|
| Non-custodial USDT rail | Money goes to your wallet |
| On-chain verification | We wait until the payment is actually there, then we mark Paid ✓ |
| Social-commerce infrastructure | For people who sell in DMs |
| Seamless checkout experience | They open a link and pay |
| Revolutionary | (delete) |
| Trusted by | (delete, or show a labeled demo screen) |
