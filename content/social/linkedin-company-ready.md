## Rejected / why

Same humanizer bar as the founder file. Company voice fails when it sounds like an agency or a launch robot.

### LC-R1 — announcement bot

**Why:** “Excited to share Pooli with the community” / “We’re live!” with no till, no toman, no Paid ✓. Banned cadence. LC-15 already has a live site and a spine.

### LC-R2 — five reasons DM sellers switch

**Why:** Listicle of obvious benefits. Could be swapped onto any checkout logo. Also invents switchers.

**Killed line:** “Here are 5 reasons sellers are switching to Pooli.”

### LC-R3 — “It’s not crypto. It’s commerce.”

**Why:** Formula. True-ish, and still a poster. LC-11 fences the category without the tic.

### LC-R4 — social-commerce thought leadership

**Why:** Three even paragraphs, generic insight, no product. “As more sales move to chat, brands need a trusted rail.” That’s how you get a TAM slide with no source.

**Killed open:** “In a world where social commerce is exploding…”
