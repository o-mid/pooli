- **Audience:** Sellers who close orders in Instagram / Telegram DMs; operators who recognize the receipt loop

- **Hook:** “I paid, can you check?”

**Text**

“I paid, can you check?”

The sale already happened. You agreed on a price in a DM. Then that message arrives, and you become a part-time accountant for your own chat.

You hunt the thread. You zoom a screenshot. The last four digits might match. The amount might match. You still aren’t sure, because a picture of a transfer is not a till.

I built Pooli for that loop. Not because payments are a category I wanted to be in. Because the receipt is the part of DM selling that never shows up on the product photo.

You send a link instead. They pay to the address you set. You see Paid ✓ when the money is actually there.

If that sounds like your evening, I’m at pooli.shop.

## Rejected / why

Humanizer pass. These drafts existed long enough to fail in public. Do not polish them back in.

### LF-R1 — five obvious benefits

**Why:** Listicle. Time / certainty / custody / language / buyer friction — all true, all from the message house, all dead on LinkedIn. Sounds like a feature table with line breaks.

**Killed line:** “Here are five things Pooli does for DM sellers.”

### LF-R2 — “It’s not crypto, it’s a till”

**Why:** The “It’s not X, it’s Y” tic. One use of “status, not a screenshot” already lives in the message house; repeating the construction as a whole post is poster copy pretending to be a person.

**Killed line:** “It’s not a wallet. It’s not an exchange. It’s a till for DMs.”

### LF-R3 — announcement bot

**Why:** Cadence of a launch email. No payload beyond “the site exists,” which LF-13 already says with a spine.

**Killed lines:** “Excited to share what we’ve been building.” / “We’re live.” / “The future of DM payments starts today.”

### LF-R4 — three short paragraphs + generic insight

**Why:** Classic LinkedIn sludge. World-has-changed opener, vague trust paragraph, soft CTA. No toman, no Paid ✓, no Instagram-off. Could be any checkout.

**Killed open:** “In a world where more commerce happens in chat, trust is the real currency.”

### LF-R5 — midnight anecdote as if it happened to me

**Why:** Positioning forbids a fake founder shop story. “Last Tuesday at 12:40 I was zooming a receipt for a customer named—” invents a night and a person. The pattern is enough. LF-01 and LF-02 already have it.

**Killed open:** “I used to sit on my bed checking card transfers until 1 a.m.”
